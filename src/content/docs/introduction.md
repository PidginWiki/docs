Pidgin.wiki is a free online reference for West African Pidgin English, spoken in
different parts of Africa including Cameroon, Nigeria, Ghana, and Sierra Leone. The aim of
the Pidgin.wiki project is to:

- Build a corpus for Pidgin English.
- Provide Pidgin word definitions and meanings.
- Understand patterns in Pidgin and propose a standard structure.
- Provide a machine model and API to translate to and from Pidgin English.
- Promote the use of Pidgin English and remove it as a barrier to education.

## Background

Four West African countries speak Pidgin English. In Nigeria alone, more than 75 million
people communicate in Pidgin ([ref](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa-38000387)). The
English-speaking regions of Cameroon account for more than 5 million fluent speakers, with
at least 2 million native speakers
([ref](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroonian_Pidgin_English)); with partial speakers
included, the figure passes 10 million in Cameroon. Ghana has at least 5 million native
speakers ([ref](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghanaian_Pidgin_English)). Adding Equatorial
Guinea, Sierra Leone, and the wider diaspora, well over 100 million Africans speak and
understand some form of Pidgin English.

With so many people speaking Pidgin English, why do computers still not understand it?

- Why do the best translation systems that exist fail on Pidgin English?
- Why is education rarely delivered in Pidgin English?
- Why is it even outlawed in certain institutions within speaking nations?

We could not find answers that justify the status quo. Pidgin English should be far more
applicable in technology and education, and we have set out to make that happen.

## Prior development efforts

Before this project, other work pursued some of these goals with varying, mostly limited,
success. See:

- [naijalingo.com](http://naijalingo.com/) (Nigerian Pidgin English dictionary)
- [BBC News Pidgin](https://www.bbc.com/pidgin) (news served in Pidgin English)

As an open source organisation, we are building a set of tools to work toward these goals,
including:

- **Pidgin.Wiki** — a free reference presenting combined dictionary, translator, and
  encyclopedia content in Pidgin English.
- **Bandolo** — a data collection and management bot.
- **Langwa** — the [neural machine translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_machine_translation) core.
